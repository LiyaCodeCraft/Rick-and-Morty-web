Multiverse Story & Lore

Premise: Rick Sanchez, a cynical mad genius scientist, drags his timid grandson Morty Smith on chaotic adventures across parallel universes and dangerous alien worlds.

Dimension C-137: The main timeline universe where our Rick originates. Known for infinite alternate timelines and reality-hopping portal travel.

The Citadel of Ricks: A secret megacity in deep space populated entirely by infinite alternate-universe versions of Rick and Morty.
